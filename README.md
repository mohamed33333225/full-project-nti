📄 UK Train Rides Data Analysis - Project Documentation1. Project OverviewIn this project, I performed an end-to-end data analysis on the UK Train Rides dataset (UK Train Rides new.csv, containing 31,653 entries). The primary goal was to extract Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), analyze passenger behavior, evaluate train punctuality, and identify peak routes to build actionable insights for executive decision-making.   The analysis was executed in Google Colab using Python with pandas for data manipulation, alongside matplotlib and seaborn for visualization.   2. Data Cleaning & Feature EngineeringTo prepare the dataset for analysis, I performed the following data preparation steps:   Handling Missing Values:Imputed missing entries in the Railcard column with 'None' to represent standard non-discounted tickets.   Data Type Conversions:Converted purchase and journey dates (Date of Purchase, Date of Journey) into standard datetime format.   Converted departure, scheduled arrival, and actual arrival times (Departure Time, Arrival Time, Actual Arrival Time) into timedelta objects to enable mathematical operations.   Feature Creation:Duration: Calculated as scheduled arrival time minus departure time (Arrival Time - Departure Time).   Delay Time: Calculated as actual arrival time minus scheduled arrival time (Actual Arrival Time - Arrival Time).   Booking Lead Time (Days): Derived as the difference in days between the journey date and purchase date (Date of Journey - Date of Purchase).   3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)After data transformation, I computed the core metrics for the network:   Average Ticket Price: £23.44   Total Revenue: £741,921.00   Overall Average Delay: 3.25 minutes (excluding cancelled flights/trips).   Average Delay (Delayed Trips Only): 42.21 minutes.   4. Visualizations & Analytical Insights1. Daily Trips Trend (Line Chart)Visualization: Time-series line chart displaying daily ride counts.   Insight: Steady daily travel demand fluctuating mostly between 240 and 300 trips per day. Significant sharp drops (outliers) occurred on specific dates (e.g., March 1st and April 1st), likely due to scheduled track maintenance or strike actions.2. Railcard Usage Distribution (Pie Chart)Visualization: Proportional pie chart categorizing railcard usage.   Insight:66.1% of passengers purchase full-price tickets without discounts (None).   15.3% utilize Adult Railcards.   Disabled and Senior Railcards account for 9.8% and 8.8% respectively.   3. Booking Lead Time Distribution (Bar Chart)Visualization: Bar chart mapping the number of days between ticket booking and actual travel date.Insight: The vast majority of bookings occur on the same day ($0$ days) or within $1$–$2$ days prior to travel. Advance bookings (7+ days prior) are minimal, highlighting an immediate-travel behavior pattern.4. Top 5 Popular Routes (Horizontal Bar Chart)Visualization: Horizontal bar chart ranking combined origin and destination pairs.   Insight:The highest-volume route is Manchester Piccadilly -> Liverpool Lime Street with 4,628 trips.   London hub stations (Euston, Kings Cross, Paddington, St Pancras) dominate the remaining top route spots.   5. Journey Status Distribution (Pie Chart)Visualization: Proportion of overall trip punctuality status.   Insight:86.8% of all scheduled trips arrived On Time.   7.2% experienced Delays.   5.9% were Cancelled, presenting an operational area for refund and reliability
